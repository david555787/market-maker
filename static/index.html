Market Maker

How it works

You are a market maker. Every round you post two prices: a bid (what you pay to buy) and an ask (what you charge to sell).

  • The asset has a hidden true value V. You only find out what it is when the game ends.
  • The game lasts 20 rounds. In each round one trader arrives and buys at your ask, sells at your bid, or does nothing.
  • Some traders are informed: they know V and only trade when your quote is wrong. The others are noise traders who trade on random hunches. You are never told which is which until the end.
  • Your inventory can never go beyond 10 units long or short. At the end your inventory is settled at V, and your final PnL is your score.
